why teams switch

The queue you stop thinking about.

Perch takes the parts of background work that bite you in production and makes them the default, not a config you forgot to set.

See every run, not just the failures.

Live logs, arguments, timing, and retries for every execution, all searchable, with alerts when latency drifts. Observability isn't a paid add-on.

Retries with real backoff.

Exponential backoff, jitter, and dead-letter handling out of the box. Set attempts: 5 and move on. Perch won't hammer a flaky API.

Schedule in plain English.

“weekdays at 9am”, “every 15 minutes”, or a raw cron string if you prefer. Perch parses it, shows you the next ten runs, and warns on ambiguity.
