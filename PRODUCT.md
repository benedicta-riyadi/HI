# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Backend and full-stack developers (and small platform teams) who need to run
background jobs, queues, and scheduled tasks but don't want to operate the
infrastructure that usually comes with them — no Redis to babysit, no cron box
to SSH into, no dead-letter queue to reinvent. They arrive from a docs link, a
teammate's recommendation, or a "how do I run this nightly?" search, evaluating
in minutes whether this is worth a `npm install`.

## Product Purpose

Perch runs background jobs that just work. You define a job in your codebase,
schedule it with plain cron or natural language, and Perch handles execution,
retries with sane backoff, observability, and scaling. Success looks like a
developer shipping a reliable scheduled task in their first sitting and never
thinking about the queue again.

## Brand Personality

Warm, playful, and quietly expert — the senior engineer who makes a hard thing
feel easy and cracks a joke while doing it. Voice is plainspoken and specific,
never hypey. Confident enough to show real code instead of talking around it.
Three words: friendly, precise, unbothered.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages on a cream/sand/paper background.
- The purple/violet AI-gradient hero with a glassmorphic card.
- "Edgy" all-black terminal devtool clones that mistake darkness for credibility.
- The hero-metric template (giant number + tiny label + supporting stats).
- Identical icon-heading-text card grids repeated down the page.

## Design Principles

- **Make the hard stuff feel calm.** Warmth and clarity over urgency and hype.
- **Show real code, not abstractions.** A working snippet beats three adjectives.
- **Playful, never cutesy.** Personality lives in copy and color, not mascots.
- **Earn trust with specifics.** Name the retry behavior, the latency, the price.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1, visible focus states
on every interactive element, full keyboard operability. Honor
`prefers-reduced-motion` with non-animated alternatives. Don't encode meaning
in color alone (pair status color with text/icon).
